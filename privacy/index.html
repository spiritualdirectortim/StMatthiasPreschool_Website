Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information.

Last updated November 5, 2026.

Summary

A simple website that collects very little.

This website shares information about St. Matthias Preschool. It has no accounts, no forms and no advertising.

Who we are

This website is operated by St. Matthias Preschool, 533 Canyon Road, Redwood City, CA 94062, a ministry of St. Matthias Catholic Church in the Archdiocese of San Francisco.

Information this website collects

You can read every page without giving us any personal information. The website does not ask you to create an account or fill out a form, and it does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Like most websites, the service that hosts these pages may automatically record basic technical information when a page is requested, such as your IP address, browser type and the page you visited, to keep the site secure and running reliably.

To display text in the site’s typefaces, pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser information when the fonts are requested. Google’s privacy policy applies to that service.

Parent videos

The parent videos are hosted on YouTube. Nothing from YouTube loads until you press play. When you do, the video plays through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), and YouTube’s privacy policy applies.

Outside services we link to

When you follow these links, you leave this website, and that service’s own privacy policy applies to any information you share there:

  • Sample parent newsletters: Google Drive.
  • Directions: Google Maps.
  • St. Matthias Parish and St. Charles School: their own websites.

Contacting the preschool

If you email or call the preschool, your message is received by preschool staff so that they can respond to you.

Children

This website does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the way this website handles information changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
