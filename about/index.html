About Us

Where children feel safe, valued and loved.

St. Matthias Preschool is a full-day, year-round Catholic preschool in Redwood City, a ministry of St. Matthias Parish and a preschool of the Archdiocese of San Francisco.

Mission & Approach

What we want for every child.

Curious minds, good friends, a sense of God’s love, and a confident first day of kindergarten.

Our mission

St. Matthias Preschool gives children a quality early education that helps them become critical thinkers and problem solvers, love learning, build good relationships, grow in Christian values and succeed in school. We do it in partnership with families, because a strong program and a strong community grow together.

How children learn here

  • Play with a purpose. Our emergent, play-based curriculum follows children’s interests, and teachers plan each activity around clear learning goals. How children learn
  • The whole child. Social-emotional growth, language and literacy, thinking skills, physical development, music and movement, STEM, gardening, Spanish, arts and crafts, and nutrition and health.
  • A plan for each child. Teachers design age-appropriate lessons around each child’s needs and interests, so every child is stretched without being rushed.
  • Ready for kindergarten. Every year builds toward the academic and social-emotional skills children need on their first day of kindergarten.

Partners with parents

Our early educators bring energy and years of experience, and they know that a child does best when home and school work together. Our partnership with families rests on mutual respect, support and trust.

Welcome from the Director

A note from Rosemary.

Rosemary Juarez-Gomez leads St. Matthias Preschool and schedules every tour. Reach her at (650) 367-1320.

Welcome to St. Matthias Preschool, where all are welcome.

We are a full-day, year-round Catholic preschool in Redwood City, and our aim is an exceptional preschool experience for every child. As a preschool of the Archdiocese of San Francisco, we serve families from St. Matthias Parish and nearby communities, and we hold our program to high standards.

Children learn best when they feel safe, valued and empowered. So we put their social and emotional well-being first, and build our play-based curriculum around it to support their academic, social-emotional and spiritual growth. Our teachers are talented and passionate, and they make our classrooms places where children can reach their potential.

We work hand in hand with parents, in a spirit of collaboration, respect and perseverance, so that every child arrives at kindergarten ready. Families join in through events, parent socials, parent workshops, parent-teacher conferences and optional volunteering, and we keep you informed through conversations with teachers, weekly emails, the Brightwheel app and a monthly newsletter.

Please come and visit. I am confident you will find St. Matthias to be a place where your child will thrive and feel loved.

Blessings,
Rosemary Juarez-GomezDirector, St. Matthias Preschool

Part of St. Matthias Parish

Rooted in a parish family.

The preschool is a ministry of St. Matthias Catholic Church, the parish in the Emerald Hills of Redwood City.

St. Matthias is a Roman Catholic community in the Archdiocese of San Francisco, striving to respond to the call of Jesus through worship and sacraments, faith formation, service, and social opportunities.

Preschool families are part of that community. The parish’s Welcome Ambassadors reach out to new preschool families, and everyone is welcome at Mass and parish events.
