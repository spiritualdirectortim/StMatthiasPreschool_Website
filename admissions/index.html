How to Enroll

Three steps to a first day.

Tours and enrollment both start with our director.

  1. Take a tour. Call or email Rosemary to visit the classrooms and playground.
  2. Talk it through. Ask about openings in your child’s age group, your start date and anything else on your mind.
  3. Register. Rosemary explains how to register, including the $575 annual registration fee.

Questions Parents Ask

Answers before you ask.

Don’t see yours? Call (650) 367-1320. We’re glad to help.

What ages do you serve?

Children ages 2 to 5. Two-year-olds are in the toddler class, three- and four-year-olds are in preschool, and pre-kindergarten is the year before kindergarten.

See programs by age

What are your hours?

Monday through Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., all year. We are closed on weekends. Ask on your tour for this year’s closure dates.

How much is tuition?

$2,550 a month for two-year-olds and $2,150 a month for ages 3 to 5, plus a $575 annual registration fee. Paying the full year up front saves 5%.

See tuition details

How do you handle toilet training?

Toilet training is part of our toddler curriculum. You and your child’s teacher decide together when to start, then follow one consistent plan at home and at school.

How toilet training works

Is the preschool only for Catholic families?

The preschool serves families from St. Matthias Parish and nearby communities, and our director’s welcome to every family begins with “where all are welcome.” Faith is part of the day in age-appropriate ways, through simple prayers and lessons in kindness, patience, respect and gratitude.

About faith and values

How will I know how my child is doing?

Through ongoing conversations with your child’s teacher, parent-teacher conferences, weekly emails with your class’s curriculum plan and snack menu, the Brightwheel app and a monthly parent newsletter.

How we stay in touch

What do children do beyond the core curriculum?

Music & Movement with a certified music teacher, monthly STEM sessions with Bricks 4 Kidz, new Spanish words each month, gardening, and age-appropriate faith formation.

Explore enrichment

Where do children go after pre-K?

Families choose the kindergarten that is right for them. St. Matthias is a feeder school for St. Charles School in San Carlos, and we encourage pre-K families to visit.

About St. Charles School

After Preschool

Looking ahead to kindergarten.

A Catholic K–8 school nearby, ready for the next step.

St. Matthias Preschool is a feeder school for St. Charles School, a coed Catholic school for kindergarten through eighth grade in San Carlos. St. Charles serves families of St. Charles Parish and the surrounding community, with a love of lifelong learning, Gospel values and a drive for academic excellence.

We encourage pre-kindergarten families to visit St. Charles and learn more.
