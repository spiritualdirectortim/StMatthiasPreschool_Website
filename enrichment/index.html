Enrichment

Music, STEM, Spanish, gardening and faith.

Five parts of the year that help children focus, build confidence and learn to work as a team, while having a very good time.

Music & Movement STEM with Bricks 4 Kidz Spanish Gardening Faith & Values

Music & Movement

Songs, rhythm and a lot of marching.

Led by Ms. Julie (Mrs. Julie Berk), a certified music instructor, for every age group.

Preschool and pre-K

Ms. Julie brings real professional experience to every session. She uses songs and simple instruments to build creativity, coordination and a love of music.

Along the way, children work on balance, memory, language, imagination and thinking skills, and learn teamwork and the pure joy of expressing themselves.

Toddlers

Toddlers clap, march, dance and jump to a rhythm, which burns energy and builds muscle strength. Instruments, action songs and lyrics help them pay attention, stay focused and learn new words, including words for how they move.

STEM with Bricks 4 Kidz

Build it, test it, try again.

Monthly on-site sessions by age group, led by Mrs. Farrah Javed of Bricks 4 Kidz.

Preschool and pre-K

Children take on projects in science, technology, engineering and math, building with LEGO bricks. Older children try robotics.

Each project sparks curiosity and careful thinking, and teaches children to solve problems logically, by trial and error. It works because the problem is in their hands, not on a worksheet.

Toddlers

Toddler STEM is about curiosity and wonder rather than tools. Children test what sinks and what floats, build with blocks to learn how things stand up, and spot simple patterns.

Spanish

A few new words every month.

Small doses, repeated often, tied to what the class is learning.

Each month, children learn five or six new Spanish words that connect to their classroom theme. They practice at circle time and through hands-on activities, repetition, pictures and games.

Meeting a second language early can support mental flexibility, literacy, memory and an awareness of other cultures.

Gardening

Their own garden, their own job.

Science they can see, smell, touch and taste.

In the preschool garden, children connect with nature through hands-on experiments, learn to care for the environment and take responsibility for what they grow.

Along the way they explore observation and change, nutrition, textures, shapes and colors, use their senses of sight, smell and taste, and build fine and gross motor skills.

Be ready to hear about every sprout.

Faith & Values

Faith they can understand.

Age-appropriate, and part of everyday learning.

Children pray together and learn the values that matter for life: caring, kindness, patience, respect and gratitude, all while coming to know the love of God.

Faith and academics often meet. How many beads are on a rosary? That is a counting lesson, too.

As a ministry of St. Matthias Parish, the preschool belongs to a wider faith community. About St. Matthias Parish
