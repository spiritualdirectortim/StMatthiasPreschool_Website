Enrichment

Music, STEM, Spanish, gardening and faith.

Through extracurricular activities, St. Matthias Preschool children discover their passion projects while engaged in learning experiences to improve their focus, develop confidence, engage in team-working experiences and enhance their academic, social and emotional performances, all while having FUN!

Music & Movement STEM with Bricks 4 Kidz Spanish Gardening Faith & Values

Music & Movement

Songs, rhythm and a lot of marching.

Led by Ms. Julie (Mrs. Julie Berk), a certified music instructor, for every age group.

Music & Movement For Preschoolers

St. Matthias Preschoolers engage in music and movement sessions led by Ms. Julie, a certified music instructor, who brings a wealth of professional experience using songs and simple instruments to foster children’s creativity, coordination and a love for music.

Through music and movement, preschoolers are able to enhance skills like balance, coordination, memory, language, creativity, imagination, cognitive functions and embrace pure joy through emotional expression and team work.

Music & Movement With Toddlers

Toddlers enjoy music and movement facilitated by their certified music teacher, Ms. Julie. Through age-appropriate music sessions, they learn simple actions like clapping, marching, dancing, and jumping to a rhythm to release energy and build muscle strength. Toddlers explore instruments, action songs and lyrics to help support their development of attention and focus, build vocabulary, including words to describe movement. These musical activities allow them to express themselves while also developing a sense of their little selves.

STEM with Bricks 4 Kidz

Build it, test it, try again.

Monthly on-site sessions by age group, led by Mrs. Farrah Javed of Bricks 4 Kidz.

STEM Sessions For Preschoolers And Pre-Kindergartners

Through Bricks 4 Kidz STEM sessions, children engage in projects to develop an understanding for the basic concepts in science, technology, engineering and math. These projects foster essential skills like encouraging natural curiosity, including critical, analytical and organizational thinking to enable them to solve problems logically through trial and error.

St. Matthias Preschool children attend monthly onsite STEM sessions according to age groups. Lego products are used to provide a fun, hands-on learning and building experiences (including robotics for our older children) focused on STEM concepts.

STEM (Toddler Sessions)

STEM sessions engage toddlers’ senses through playful and sensorial exploration and simple explanations. These sessions concentrate on cultivating a mindset of curiosity and wonder rather than a specific set of tools. Toddlers can learn cause and effect with experiments like sink or float, build with blocks to understand engineering, and recognize very simple patterns in math. These activities focus on sensory experiences and problem-solving through fun and age-appropriate methods.

Spanish

A few new words every month.

Small doses, repeated often, tied to what the class is learning.

Each month, children are introduced to 5-6 new Spanish words corresponding to their curriculum focus. Through simple circle times, hands-on activities, repetition, visuals and games make learning Spanish more engaging and dynamic at St. Matthias Preschool.

Early exposure to a second language helps preschoolers develop greater mental flexibility, stronger literacy skills, enhanced memory and increased cultural awareness.

Gardening

Their own garden, their own job.

Science they can see, smell, touch and taste.

Through gardening, our young learners connect to nature while engaging in exciting sensorial experiences concentrated on scientific experimentation, environmental awareness and being responsible for their garden. Children also explore other integrative learning concepts including observation, transformation, nutrition, textures, shapes, colors, sight, smell, taste and fine & gross motor development.

Faith & Values

Faith they can understand.

Age-appropriate, and part of everyday learning.

Through Age Appropriate Faith Formation, we foster prayer and teach about the important values in life related to caring, kindness, patience, respect and gratitude while learning the love of God.

How many beads are on a Rosary? (academic learning through Age Appropriate Faith Formation)

As a ministry of St. Matthias Parish, the preschool belongs to a wider faith community. About St. Matthias Parish
