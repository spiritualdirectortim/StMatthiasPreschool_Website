Staying in Touch

You’ll always know what’s happening.

What to expect once your child is enrolled.

Every week
An email with your class’s curriculum plan and snack menu.
Brightwheel app
Preschool news, events and updates on your child’s progress.
Every month
A parent newsletter with preschool news and upcoming events.
Conferences
Parent-teacher conferences about your child’s growth.
Every day
Ongoing conversations with your child’s teacher.

Want to help out? Volunteering is always optional and always appreciated. Ask your child’s teacher how to get involved.

Family Events

Mornings your children plan for you.

Some of the best events of the year are hosted by preschoolers.

Throughout the year, the children invite their parents into their classrooms for events they host themselves. Recent ones include:

  • Mommy & Me Lemonade Tea Party, for Mother’s Day
  • Mommy Spa Party, for Mother’s Day
  • Breakfast & Building with Daddy, for Father’s Day
  • Daddy & Me Photo Puzzle and Morning Snack, for Father’s Day

Parent socials

Parents get gatherings of their own, too, like the Halloween and Christmas parent socials, which parent representatives on the Preschool Advisory Board have helped host. They are a relaxed way to get to know other preschool families.

Parent Workshops

Expert help with the hard parts.

Two to three times a year, from 4:00 to 5:15 p.m., while your child is still in care.

St. Matthias Preschool hires reputable, certified professionals to lead parent workshops, which we call parent in-services. Parents choose the topics, and each presentation is built around them. Past topics include:

  • Getting your child ready for kindergarten
  • Setting boundaries and handling power struggles
  • Separation anxiety
  • Recognizing signs of depression in preschoolers
  • Sibling rivalry

Workshops happen during program hours, so you can attend without arranging a babysitter.
