Our mission

Children here learn to think for themselves, get along with others, know they are loved by God, and walk into kindergarten ready.

St. Matthias Preschool is a ministry of St. Matthias Parish and a preschool of the Archdiocese of San Francisco, serving families from the parish and nearby communities.

Programs by age

A classroom for each stage, from first words to first letters.

Each teacher plans around the children in the room: their age, their interests, and what they are ready to learn next.

Outdoor classroom

A playground the size of a park.

Tall shade trees, a lower yard, climbing structures and paths for bikes and scooters. Outside, children run, balance, dig and draw, building the strength and coordination growing bodies need.

Expect a little dirt on the knees at pickup. That is the curriculum working.
