Through **Bricks 4 Kidz** STEM sessions, children engage in projects to develop an understanding for the basic concepts in science, technology, engineering and math.  These projects foster essential skills like encouraging natural curiosity, including critical, analytical and organizational thinking to enable them to solve problems logically through trial and error.

St. Matthias Preschool children attend monthly onsite STEM sessions according to age groups.  Lego products are used to provide a fun, hands-on learning and building experiences (including robotics for our older children) focused on STEM concepts.
