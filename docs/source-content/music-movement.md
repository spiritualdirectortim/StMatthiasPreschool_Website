# MUSIC & MOVEMENT

St. Matthias Preschoolers engage in music and movement sessions led by Ms. Julie, a certified music instructor, who brings a wealth of professional experienc using songs and simple instruments to foster children's creativity, coordination and a love for music. (sic: "experienc")

Through music and movement, preschoolers are able to enhance skills like balance, coordination, memory, language, creativity, imagination, cognitive functions and embrace pure joy through emotional expression and team work.
