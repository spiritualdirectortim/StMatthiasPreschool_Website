St. Matthias Preschool hires reputable and certified professionals to design and deliver customized Parent In-Service presentations to preschool parents based on the training topics selected by them. (Preparing Your Child For Kindergarten Readiness / Setting Boundaries & Power Struggles / Separation Anxiety, Detecting Signs of Depression In Preschoolers / Sibling Rivalry / Etc.).

These Parent In-Services are hosted two to three times per preschool year and are held during program hours (4:00-5:15 P.M.) to enable parents to attend while their children are still in care at preschool.
