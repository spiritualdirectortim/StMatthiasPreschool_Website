# GARDENING

Through gardening, our young learners connect to nature while engaging in exciting sensorial experiences concentrated on scientific experimentation, environmental awareness and being responsible for their garden.  Children also explore other integrative learning concepts including observation, transformation, nutrition, textures, shapes, colors, sight, smell, taste and fine & gross motor development.
