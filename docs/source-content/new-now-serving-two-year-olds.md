St. Matthias Preschool embraces Toddler toilet training as an important early childhood learning concept to help our youngest acquire the skills to stand, sit, balance, and weight shift while occupying a potty chair or toilet, all of which are implemented into our daily curriculum.

In partnership, parents and teacher will:

- Determine when the toddler exhibits readiness for toilet training.
- Create a collaborative and consistent toilet training plan to ensure mutual support for the toddler to build confidence, reduce stress and achieve a successful toilet training experience.

Please contact our program for more details.
