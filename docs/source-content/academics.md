# CURRICULUM

Each classroom teacher creates a curriculum design based on children's developmental stages, interests, individual needs and strengths. Through an inclusive and child-centered approach we provide intentional teachable moments and activities that introduce new concepts and skills in a systematic way.

During children's learning engagements, teachers observe and track their developmental progress and understanding of new concepts and proceed to further expand their scope of learning.

Weekly, parents receive, via email, their classroom **Curriculum Plan** and **Snack Menu.**

For more details, please refer to age appropriate curriculum links listed in this website
