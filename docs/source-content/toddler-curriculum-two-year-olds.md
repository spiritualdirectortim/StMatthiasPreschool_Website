# TODDLER CURRICULUM (TWO YEAR OLDS)

Our toddler curriculum centers on play-based and experience-rich learning opportunities surrounding basic concepts (shapes, colors, etc.) through sensory-based activities, outdoor exploration, playful experimentation and engaging in simple conversations, which include narrating daily activities to build language.

Through responsive caregiving and secure child-teacher relationships, toddlers feel safe and emotionally supported to confidently explore their learning environments.
