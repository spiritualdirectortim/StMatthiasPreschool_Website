# SPANISH

Each month, children are introduced to 5-6 new Spanish words corresponding to their curriculum focus.  Through simple circle times, hands-on activities, repetition, visuals and games make learning Spanish more engaging and dynamic at St. Matthias Preschool.

Early exposure to a second language helps preschoolers develop greater mental flexibility, stronger literacy skills, enhanced memory and increased cultural awareness.
