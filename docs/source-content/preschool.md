# PRESCHOOL

At St. Matthias Preschool children are empowered through an Emergent play-based curriculum to make their own choices about play, take risks, solve problems, and use their imagination in open-ended activities. Through their learning journey, teachers play an instrumental role in supporting their understanding by providing them with hands-on experiences and promoting opportunities for purposeful play and meaningful interactions. Such experiences enable our young explorers to develop in overall growth areas such as: self-direction, independence, social-emotional regulation, critical thinking, problem-solving, cognitive skills, language and physical abilities.
