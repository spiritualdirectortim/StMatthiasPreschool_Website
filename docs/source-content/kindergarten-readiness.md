# Pre-Kindergarten

Experienced early educators provide nurturing and instrumental strategies within a structured, fun and robust curriculum focused on key academic concepts and social-emotional learning to prepare Pre-Kindergarteners for "Kindergarten Readiness."

Pre-Kindergarteners strategically work in small groups throughout the preschool year to master skill acquisition focused on the following learning domains of their Pre-Kindergarten Assessment comparable to Transitional Kindergarten (TK) objectives:

- Cognitive (early math/classification/patterning/other STEM focused concepts).
- Early Literacy Phonological Awareness.
- Language.
- Fine & Gross Motor.
- Social-Emotional.
- Work Habits
- Routines
