# MUSIC & MOVEMENT (TODDLERS)

Toddlers enjoy music and movement facilitated by their certified music teacher, Ms. Julie.  Through age-appropriate music sessions, they learn simple actions like clapping, marching, dancing, and jumping to a rhythm to release energy and build muscle strength.  Toddlers explore instruments, action songs and lyrics to help support their development of attention and focus, build vocabulary, including words to describe movement. These musical activities allow them to express themselves while also developing a sense of their little selves.
