Welcome To St. Matthias Preschool Where All Are Welcome!

St. Matthias Preschool is a full day, year-round Catholic early education and care program located in Redwood City, CA where we are committed to ensure an exceptional preschool experience for all children.

As a preschool of the Archdiocese of San Francisco, serving families within the St. Matthias Parish and nearby communities, we remain dedicated to maintaining high quality program standards and providing an enriched play-based curriculum that promotes critical thinking, creativity, and a love for learning.

We understand the importance of fostering a supportive and inclusive preschool climate where children feel safe, valued, and empowered. Our program prioritizes the social and emotional well-being of our preschoolers through our creative curriculum masterfully designed to support their academic, social-emotional and spiritual development.

Our talented and passionate teachers engage children in innovative early childhood teaching methods that promote opportunity for growth in our stimulating and nurturing learning environments driven to help children reach their potential.

Through parent partnerships, we embrace the spirit of collaboration, respect, and perseverance, working hand in hand to support children's early learning experiences to successfully become Kindergarten Ready.

We value the importance of fostering a connection between the classroom and the home to establish a strong preschool community that encourages families to participate through our various events, parent socials, parent in-services, parent-teacher conferences, and optional volunteering opportunities.

Communication between staff and parents is channeled through on-going parent-teacher dialogue, weekly emails, the Brightwheel App, monthly Parent Newsletters and our preschool website as a means of keeping our families informed about important preschool news, events, and children's progress.

Please visit our vibrant and diverse preschool community. We are confident that you will find St. Matthias Preschool to be a place where your child will thrive and feel loved in the process.

Blessings,
Rosemary Juarez
Director of St. Matthias Preschool
