STEM sessions engage toddlers' senses through playful and sensorial exploration and simple explanations. These sessions concentrate on cultivating a mindset of curiosity and wonder rather than a specific set of tools.  Toddlers can learn cause and effect with experiments like sink or float, build with blocks to understand engineering, and recognize very simple patterns in math.  These activities focus on sensory experiences and problem-solving through fun and age-appropriate methods.
