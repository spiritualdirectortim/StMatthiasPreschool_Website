# OUTDOOR LEARNING ENVIRONMENT

In our enormous, park-like playground, children immerse into sensory-rich experiences and physical activity their rapidly developing minds and bodies need. Our early educators create stimulating outdoor spaces to foster children's motor development, enhance their strength, endurance, flexibility and balance, all while promoting exploration and an appreciation for nature.
