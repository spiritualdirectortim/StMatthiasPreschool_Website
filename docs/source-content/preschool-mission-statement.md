St. Matthias Preschool is committed and driven to provide a quality early education experience which inspires children to become critical thinkers, problem solvers, develop a passion for learning, establish successful relationships, acquire Christian values and achieve academic success while building partnerships with families to enrich our overall program and community in the process.

Our Emergent play-based, academic curriculum promotes children's growth across a broad range of developmental and content areas which include:

social-emotional, language and literacy, cognitive, physical, music & movement, STEM, gardening, Spanish, arts and crafts, nutrition and health.

Our professional staff design an individualized and age appropriate curriculum which fosters optimal learning based on the developmental needs and interests of children to maximize their academic and social- emotional potential necessary for Kindergarten Readiness.

Our energetic, enthusiastic and seasoned early educators are fueled with a passion and recognize the importance of establishing and maintaining supportive partnerships with families based on mutual respect, support and trust which is essential to help each child achieve their overall success.
